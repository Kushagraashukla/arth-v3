Arth use karna, 6 aasaan steps mein

Kisi bhi website par koi word ya line samajh na aaye, to wahin uska matlab dekho. Na naya tab, na naya chat. Jab aap pehli baar use karoge, yahi steps card ke paas chhote tips ban kar live dikhenge.

  1. Word ya line select karo

    Kisi bhi page par (Wikipedia, ChatGPT, news, docs) mouse se word select karo. Neeche do button aayenge: Arth aur Ask.

    …elements that remain invariant when raised to a positive power…
    ArthAsk

  2. "Arth" dabao, matlab wahin pao

    Card mein us word ka matlab aayega, usi jagah ke context mein, dictionary wala generic nahi. Ask dabao to apna sawaal likh sakte ho.

    ARTHLaymanHinglishSave
    Yahan "raised" ka matlab hai kisi number ko power dena, jaise 2 ko 3rd power tak…

  3. Style aur bhasha apne hisaab se

    Card ke upar do menu hain. Style: Concise, Layman, Simple + Analogy, Deep Dive, Technical, Exam Notes. Bhasha: English, Hinglish, हिंदी. Badalte hi answer isi card mein naye style mein dobara ban jata hai, aur aage ke liye yaad rehta hai.

    ARTHExam Notesहिंदी
    Definition: …
    Key points: …
    Revision: …

  4. Answer ke andar ka word bhi

    Answer mein koi word samajh na aaye? Use select karo aur chhota Arth chip dabao. Uska matlab usi card mein neeche khulta hai. Back se wapas, Close se band.

    …number ko power dena… Arth
    power
    Kisi number ko khud se kitni baar multiply karna hai, ye batana.

  5. Save karo, baad mein yaad rahega

    Save dabao. Jab aap is page par wapas aaoge, wo word halka sa highlight dikhega. Us par mouse le jao (ya tap karo) to saved matlab wahin dikh jayega.

    …remain invariant when raised to a positive power…
    raised
    Kisi number ko power dena, jaise a^2…

  6. Apne saare words, ek jagah

    Card mein Words dabao (ya extension ke Options). Saare saved words, unke matlab, search, aur apne notes. Toolbar ke Arth icon se poore page ke baare mein bhi pooch sakte ho.

    raisedKisi number ko power dena…
    powerKitni baar multiply karna hai…
