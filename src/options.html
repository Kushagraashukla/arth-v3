Arth

Understand what you're reading.

What Arth does

While you read a conversation on chatgpt.com, select a word or short phrase and click the small “Arth” button that appears next to it. Arth shows a short explanation of what that text means in that passage.

What Arth sends, and where

Nothing is sent until you click the Arth button. When you click it, Arth sends this to the OpenAI API:

  • the text you selected (up to 300 characters);
  • the text around it in the same message (up to about 1,500 characters);
  • up to three neighbouring messages from the same conversation (up to 500 characters each).

It goes directly from your browser to OpenAI, using your own OpenAI API key, and is handled under OpenAI’s API terms and privacy policy. Arth has no server of its own. Arth does not keep your selections or the explanations, and has no analytics or tracking.

Connect your OpenAI API key

Arth uses the OpenAI API, which is separate from a ChatGPT subscription and is billed by OpenAI to your API account. A typical explanation costs a small fraction of a cent.

  1. Create a key at platform.openai.com/api-keys.
  2. Paste it below. It is stored only in this browser and is sent only to api.openai.com.

“Check key” asks OpenAI which models your key can use and fills this list.

Your control

  • “Remove key and settings” deletes everything Arth has saved. You can also revoke the key at OpenAI at any time.
  • Press Escape or click elsewhere to close an explanation; a request still in progress is cancelled.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome removes its saved data.
